Passion Double Cone Blender most often used for the intimate dry blending of free-flowing solids.basic machine with two cone blender volume for optimum homogeneity is between 30-75% of gross volume - depend on powder /granules.


The Double - Two shape blenders machines have long two corners of materials product container for more rubbing & tumbler effect for fast and homogenizing products mixing. It can perform products materials homogeneously dry mixing of free-flowing blending powders, granules requiring low shearing force, powder Mixing and Blending of particles with rapid inter-mixing of particles irrespective of varying specific gravities of products.


The product's material is loaded into the double cone blender drum and cone both side special type knows type fitting given. The double blender is double cone - tumbles the material in the container and spread out and quick easily operable flange fitting given to operator can open and take the blended product. The products mixing is achieved in 5 to 20 minutes with better homogeneity depend on powder/granules.
