Brochure

  • Product_Brochure

Passion Underdriven comil provides all the features and options of the original comil with additional advantages, including easier inline integration. This mill’s advanced, compact design and extremely short head height provide for easy integration into processing environments where head room is limited. A large-diameter infeed and “straight through” housing (with no offset between inlet and outlet) promote unhindered inline product flow for quicker discharge .Available in five models, the Underdriven comil is scalable in capacity from 15 to 1000Kg/Hr.


Fine tuning the mill for optimum granulometry is simplified by means of an innovative spaceless design, which allows for quick changeover of impellers and screens. Specialized tooling is available to meet all process requirements. A variable-speed feature provides for even greater control of process performance.Machine Capacities are based on standard pharmaceutical placebo may vary accordingly based on product characteristics, particle granulometry, inlet and discharge conditions.



Features



  • Extremely short housing height for installation in production environments where space is limited
  • Spaceless design provides, optimum impeller/screen
  • Gap for superior particle granulometry and performance
  • Minimum surface area resulting in maximum product flow and lower cleaning costs
  • Inlet and outlet can be customized to fit any process conditions
  • Safety features to meet all International
  • Guidelines and Standards
  • Quick changeover of screens and impellers, no special tools required
  • CIP capability available

In principle all sizes of our Cone Mill (Underdriven) are available.
