Passion Automation & Engineering High Shear Mixer Granulator is a high shear machine, developed for mixing as well as granulation. The multibladed dual speed impeller and chopper performs the mixing and breaking of materials.


Unique design incorporating Impeller, Granulator, Online Co-mill, Side discharge valve and dryer


Internal and External surfaces of Mixing Bowl, Mixing Bowl cover, Impeller, Granulator, and Discharge Chute constructed in SS 304/316L


Specially mounted torque sensor arrangement for critical parameters of granulation like end point determination and desired granule profile



Impeller Lifting Options



  • Motorized Impeller Lifting for ease of cleaning
  • Electromechanical impeller lifting and removing mechanism
  • Pneumatically operated Lid lifting
  • ATEX Compliant option available
