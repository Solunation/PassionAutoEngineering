Brochure

  • Product_Brochure

Boins coater has been designed to carry out uniform coating with the least intervention by the operator. It is free standing ,totally enclosed unit within built WIP system. The side and front doors are provided for easy cleaning and maintenance. The unique perforated coating pan designed with highly efficient air flow for maximum uniform drying and minimum process time. Air flow supply through the inlet duct and directed down through product bed under negative pressure through exhaust .


Boins coater are designed to handle sugar coating tablets contains an outside layer of tablet which protects the medication improves appearance and flavor


Film Coating is achieved using organic coating material or aqueous coating solutions as solvents are much more easier to evaporate compared to water based coating solutions.


Design Features

  •   Pneumatic telescopic spray nozzle arm
  •   Rabbit-ear, Tubular baffles, Plate type baffle is use as per tablet shape, coating process and customers need
  •   Multiheaded peristaltic pump with solution preparation tank
  •   Front charging with a chute
  •   provides specially designed discharge chute and discharge drum
  •   The Pan is rotated at slow RPM.Discharge chute picks up even the last single tablet
  •   Air Handling Unit section frame and thick “PUF” insulated panel having SS 304 inner sheet and outer powder coated GI sheet
  •   We option for treating exhaust air high efficient wet scrubbing for dry air filtration system
  •   WIP / CIP fully integrated with process controls
  •   Control Via PLC Touch Screen Based HMI, with necessary communication ports and printer port
  •   Coating Pan speed, Inlet air supply, Peristaltic Pump, solution preparation vessel control Through Variable Frequency AC Drive


In principle all sizes of our Auto Coater are available.
