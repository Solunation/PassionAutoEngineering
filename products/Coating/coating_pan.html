Passion Automation & Engineering are engaged in offering our esteemed clients with a qualitative range of Coating Pans, these are widely used in the process of coating tablets and confectionaries, wherein the skills of the coater need to be supplemented by a correctly designed, versatile coating pan. Our range has a very heavy & sturdy structure and is made from angles as well as channels.


The coating pan must have effective baffles to allow proper mixing of bulk tablets during coating . The pan must be fitted with a retractable arm for spray assembly which is an angle adjustable device for spray/dosing header.


Salient Features

  •   Design is cGMP Practices compliance.
  •   All contact parts AISI 316/304 & non-contact parts AISI 304.
  •   Conventional coating system for manual film coating and sugar coating.
  •   Machine is designed with fabricated structure fitted with SS enclosure which makes simple, cleanable outside covering and mounted on anti-vibration pad.
  •   Closed powerful pan drive with reduction gearbox and motor by variable speed pulley.
  •   Movable hot air blowing system with electric heater.
  •   Easy to replace polishing pan and other size coating pan.


Optional Features

  •   A.C. frequency Drive for pan
  •   Fixed or detachable type baffles
  •   Interchange different size Pan & Polishing Pan
  •   Atomized Spraying system with Spray Gun Peristaltic Pump and Solution tank and Electro Pneumatic controls
  •   Pressurized vessel with spray gun
  •   Flame Proof Electrical

In principle all sizes of our Coating Pan are available.
